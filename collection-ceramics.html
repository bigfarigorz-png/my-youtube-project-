Iran is one of the world's largest tile producers — and the glazes that made Persian tilework famous are the same blues and teals that run through Armenian craft. Wall, floor and décor, fired close to the border.

Glazed Wall Tile Stone-Look Porcelain Décor & Mosaic Also available

Two materials, one tile aisle.

"Ceramic" and "porcelain" get used interchangeably, but they do different jobs. We supply both and help you put each where it belongs.

Glazed ceramic is lighter and softer-bodied — perfect for walls, splashbacks and decoration, where its glaze can be richly coloured and patterned. Porcelain is denser, harder and barely absorbs water, which makes it the right answer for floors, wet rooms, high-traffic commercial spaces and outdoors. Most projects use both: a stone-look porcelain underfoot, a glazed feature on the wall.

02 / Porcelain
Porcelain · Floors & traffic

Stone-Look Porcelain

Rectified · large format · low absorption

The hard-wearing floor. Modern porcelain reproduces marble and travertine convincingly while outperforming them on water absorption, stain resistance and traffic. Rectified edges allow tight, near-seamless joints, and large formats — up to 60×120 and beyond — make a floor read as continuous stone. Specify an R10/R11 surface for wet and outdoor areas.
