Marble looks, porcelain performance.

Veronzo is the porcelain line we carry alongside our natural stone — the practical answer when a project wants the look of marble with the durability of tile.

Modern porcelain is dense, hard and barely absorbs water, so it handles floors, wet rooms, high-traffic commercial spaces and outdoor terraces that natural stone can't always take. Rectified edges allow tight, near-seamless joints, and large formats — up to 60×120 and beyond — let a floor or wall read as one continuous surface. Below are the main looks; the full collection runs to more than a hundred designs, free to download.

01 / Marble-look
Veronzo · The statement floor

Calacatta Gold

White ground · bold gold & grey veining

The showpiece of the collection: a bright white body crossed by dramatic gold-and-grey veins, polished to a mirror or left soft-matt. In large format it bookmatches into feature walls and waterfall islands that read as a single block of marble — without the sealing, staining and maintenance that real Calacatta demands.
