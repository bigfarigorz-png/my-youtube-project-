Marble looks, porcelain performance.

Veronzo is the porcelain line we carry alongside our natural stone — established in 2022 and fired in Iran, it gives a project the look of marble, stone or concrete with the durability only porcelain delivers.

Porcelain is dense and barely absorbs water, so it handles floors, wet rooms, high-traffic commercial spaces and outdoor terraces that natural stone can't always take. Rectified edges allow tight, near-seamless joints, and large formats — up to 120×280 and 160×320 — let a wall or floor read as one continuous surface. Polished or matt.
