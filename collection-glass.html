Building Systems · Glass & Aluminium

The glass envelope.

Where the building meets the light. Adonis supplies the full glass-and-aluminium envelope from Iran to Armenia — curtain-wall facades, thermal-break windows and doors, tempered, laminated and double-glazed units, interior glass and roll-up closures, all engineered to fit and finished to spec.

Glass Facade Thermal-Break Windows Interior Glass Also available

Skin and structure, together.

A glass facade is two trades at once — the aluminium that holds it and the glass that fills it. We supply both as one system, from accredited Iranian fabricators, so the profile, the gasket and the insulated glass unit all arrive matched and ready to install.

From a single shopfront to a full curtain-walled tower, every unit is cut to your opening schedule, glazed to the performance you need — solar control, acoustic, low-E — and crated for the road to Yerevan. European quality, supplied direct, without the middleman's mark-up.

Thermal-break · aluminium + IGU

Thermal-Break Doors & Windows

Up to 40% less heat loss

A smarter window. A polyamide insulating strip (PA6 GF25) is set into the aluminium profile to break the path heat takes through the frame — so winter warmth stays in and summer heat stays out, cutting energy use by up to 40%. Paired with double- or triple-glazed units, it's the comfort-and-efficiency standard for homes, offices and towers alike.
