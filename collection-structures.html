Building Systems · Steel & Structures

The bones of the build.

The structure under the finish. Adonis supplies galvanised composite deck sheet, space-frame roof systems, profiles and building blocks from Iran's mills to Armenian sites — standard sections, accredited steel, quoted by the tonne and delivered to Yerevan.

See the systems →

Faster floors, lighter frames.

Modern construction moves quickly because the structure arrives ready to work. Composite deck sheet turns a floor into a one-day pour; a space frame roofs a hall with almost no columns. Adonis supplies these systems direct from reputable Iranian producers, to standard or to your engineer's section.

Every load is mill-certified and cut to length, crated and documented for the border crossing. The same supplier that brings your stone and glass brings the steel underneath them.

Steel Deck Sheet

Galvanised · trapezoidal · roll-formed

One of the fastest, most economical floor systems in construction. A galvanised steel sheet is roll-formed into a trapezoidal profile that does three jobs at once — permanent formwork, structural reinforcement and a working platform — so it has all but replaced traditional composite slabs on big builds. Once it's laid over the beams, you pour straight onto it.

Space-Frame Structures

Column-free long-span roofs

A lattice of struts and nodes that roofs a huge floor with almost no columns. The triangulated double-layer grid spreads load in every direction, so it spans halls, terminals, showrooms and sports venues lightly and economically — fabricated to your geometry and bolted together on site from a kit of members.
