Warmth, engineered.

Adonis Co is the export arm of the Adonis Industrial Group — five decades of Iranian heating and home-appliance manufacturing. We bring that line to Armenia: smart gas fan-heaters and wall-hung boiler packages, built to keep a home warm through a Yerevan winter.

Five decades of heat.

Where the rest of our catalogue is quarried and refined, this one is engineered. The Adonis Industrial Group has spent more than fifty years building cooling, heating and home-appliance products in Iran — and Adonis Co carries that pedigree across the border, direct from the factory to your distributor in Armenia.

Quoted by the pallet or the container, with spare parts, manuals and after-sales support behind every unit. The same single point of contact that handles your stone and glass handles your heating line.

01 · Flagship

Smart Fan Heater.

A gas fan-heater with a brain — it runs on natural gas or LPG, pushes warm air out fast with a built-in fan, and watches itself with a smart fault-detection system. An optional remote lets the room set its own temperature from the sofa.
