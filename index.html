01 Studio

We believe architecture is the slowest art — buildings that hold light, frame silence, and age with grace.

Founded in Lisbon in 2012, the studio works between architecture, interiors and urban strategy. Every commission begins the same way: with the site, the light, and the people who will live inside the work.

From a clifftop house in the Algarve to a museum carved into a former power station, our projects share one ambition — clarity. Few materials, precise details, generous daylight.
