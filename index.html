One border, three trades, handled end‑to‑end.

Iran sits on some of the oldest stone and clay in the world — and on Armenia's doorstep. Adonis Co turns that proximity into an advantage: a single supplier for stone, brick and ceramic, bought at source and brought across the border without the markup of three middlemen.

From block selection in the quarry to a pallet on your site in Yerevan, the chain stays in one pair of hands. You approve the samples; we handle the cutting, the crating, the paperwork and the crossing.

Quarry to site

How an order reaches Yerevan.

Five steps, one supplier. The sequence is the same whether you order a single pallet of brick or a container of bookmatched slabs.

01

Source

Blocks and batches selected direct from quarries and kilns across Iran — Khorasan, Yazd, Isfahan, Fars and beyond.

02

Fabricate

Cut, calibrated and finished to your spec — slab gauge, tile format, finish and edge — sampled and approved first.

03

Inspect

Each consignment graded, photographed and labelled by block and shade, so what you approve is what arrives.

04

Export

Crated, palletised and documented — invoice, certificate of origin, packing list — on FCL or consolidated LCL freight.

05

Deliver

Customs cleared at the Armenian border and delivered to your yard, showroom or site in Yerevan and across the country.

About Adonis Co

A trading house built on one short border.

Adonis Co is a materials export business working the route between Iran and Armenia. We exist because the two countries are neighbours, and the best Persian stone, brick and ceramic should reach Armenian sites without passing through three sets of hands and three markups.

Quarry-direct pricing

We buy at source, by the container, and sell the same way. The block, the kiln batch and your invoice sit close together — no layer of resellers in between.

Specified, not guessed

Samples, finishes and gauges are approved before production. Veining and shade are matched batch to batch, so the second container looks like the first.

One supplier, three trades

Stone, brick and ceramic on a single order, a single shipment and a single point of contact — from the quarry face to your gate in Yerevan.
