01 Studio

We shape quiet, generous spaces — from Yerevan's tuff streets to the foothills of the Alborz.

Bahareh Hajizadeh is an architect and interior designer practicing between Yerevan, Tehran and Mashhad. Her studio carries projects from the first sketch to the last handle — architecture, interiors and renovation under one author's eye.

The work draws on two deep traditions — Persian geometry and craft, and the stone modernism of the Caucasus — translated into calm contemporary spaces: few materials, precise detail, generous daylight.

03 Services

From first sketch
to final handle.

  • 01

    Architectural Design

    Site analysis, concept, permit and construction documents for villas, houses and public buildings — drawings precise enough to price, details considered enough to last.

    →
  • 02

    Interior Architecture

    Interiors designed as buildings in miniature — joinery, lighting, stone and textile composed with the same rigour as structure, from plan to material board.

    →
  • 03

    Renovation & Site Supervision

    Trusted contractors, weekly site visits and author's supervision until handover — so what is built is exactly what was drawn, on budget and on schedule.

    →
  • 04

    Furniture & Styling

    Furniture, art, light and objects — sourced, commissioned or custom-made — so the space is finished, photographed and ready to live in on day one.

04 Process

Five steps,
one author's eye.

  1. 01

    Consultation & Brief

    We meet at the site or online, walk through how you live and work, and agree the scope, budget frame and timeline.

  2. 02

    Concept Design

    Plans, mood and massing — tested fast in sketches and models until the idea is unmistakably yours.

  3. 03

    Design Development

    Full drawing set: layouts, joinery, lighting, materials and specifications — precise enough to tender.

  4. 04

    Construction & Supervision

    Contractor selection, weekly site visits and author's supervision — every detail checked against the drawings.

  5. 05

    Styling & Handover

    Furniture, art and objects placed; the space photographed, documented and handed over ready to live in.
