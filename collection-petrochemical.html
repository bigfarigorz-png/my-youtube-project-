Bitumen & waterproofing.

Refinery-grade Iranian bitumen and bitumen-polymer membrane, supplied to the Armenian market by the drum, the bag and the container. Penetration-grade for asphalt and roofing, hard oxidised grade for industry, and ready-rolled Isogam for roofs and foundations that have to survive a Caucasus winter.

See the grades →

Straight from the still.

Bitumen is the heavy residue left at the bottom of the oil-refining tower — a sticky, solid hydrocarbon whose chemistry is close to natural asphalt. Iran refines some of the world's most consistent bitumen, and Adonis supplies it direct from accredited producers to contractors and waterproofing factories across Armenia.

We carry the two grades a job actually calls for — soft penetration bitumen for asphalt and built-up roofing, and hard oxidised bitumen for industry and membrane production — plus finished Isogam rolls for sites that want waterproofing ready to lay. Every load ships with its test certificate and the documents to clear the border.

Bitumen 60/70 · the workhorse

Penetration Bitumen 60/70

Soft, penetration-grade · road & roofing

The standard paving and waterproofing grade, named for its penetration value (60–70 dmm at 25 °C). Workable, adhesive and forgiving — it binds aggregate into hot-mix asphalt and forms the base layer of built-up roofing felts. The everyday grade most projects mean when they say “bitumen.”

Bitumen 90/15 · hard / blown

Oxidised Bitumen 90/15

“Hard” bitumen · high softening point

Air-blown bitumen with a high softening point — it stays solid in heat and turns fluid only around 90 °C, with a penetration of about 15. The controlled oxidation gives it a rubber-like body and high stability, with no catalyst added. The industrial grade for membrane manufacture, pipe coating, electrical and insulation work — usually cut with a softer grade so it never turns brittle.

Isogam · bitumen-polymer membrane

Isogam Waterproofing

Prefabricated torch-on rolls

Waterproofing that arrives finished: a polymer-modified bitumen membrane, reinforced with a fibre core and faced with mineral or foil, supplied in rolls and torched down on site. It wraps roofs, terraces, foundations and wet rooms in a single continuous skin — flexible enough to take a building's movement and the freeze-thaw of an Armenian winter without cracking.
