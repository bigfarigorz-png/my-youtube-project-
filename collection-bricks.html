Brick

Kiln-fired clay brick from Iran — facing brick, thin slips and glazed engineering brick. The honest, frost-hardy face of a building, in every earth tone from deep red to pale sand.

Clay Facing Brick Glazed Brick Brick Slips Also available

Clay, fired close to home.

Brick is the most forgiving material we ship and one of the most durable. Iranian clay brick is fired hard, holds its colour, and shrugs off the freeze-thaw cycle that an Armenian winter demands.

We supply full bricks for load-bearing and traditional masonry, and thin slips for cladding over insulation and modern facade systems. Colour runs are blended to your sample so a whole elevation reads as one batch, and every pallet is shrink-wrapped and corner-protected for the road north.

01 / Facing
Facing brick · The face of the build

Clay Facing Brick

Extruded (wire-cut) & handmade

The everyday hero: a hard-fired clay brick for external walls and feature elevations. Wire-cut faces give a crisp, uniform line; handmade faces give a soft, textured, slightly irregular character for a more traditional look. Natural colour spreads from deep red and rust through terracotta to warm sand, blended to your chosen mix.

02 / Glazed
Glazed · Colour & density

Glazed Engineering Brick

Semi-gloss glaze · low absorption

A denser, finer brick finished with a fired glaze — caramel and toffee from stock, or made to a colour. The glaze gives a wipe-clean, semi-gloss face and very low water absorption, so it suits plinths, wet zones and bold accent banding as well as full elevations. Strong, dense and crisp-edged.

03 / Slips & systems
Brick slips · Cladding

Thin Brick Slips

≈ 20–25 mm thin cladding tiles

All the look of a brick wall at a fraction of the weight and depth. Slips are thin slices of the same fired clay, bonded to insulated facade systems, lift cores and interior features where a full brick won't fit. Matching corner pieces wrap external angles so the illusion of solid masonry is complete.
