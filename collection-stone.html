Why Iranian stone.

Iran is one of the world's great stone countries — vast reserves, hundreds of working quarries, and varieties that the trade has prized for generations. The advantage for an Armenian project is simple geography: this material is next door, not an ocean away.

We supply stone two ways — as gangsaw slabs for fabricators who cut to size, and as calibrated tiles and cut-to-size pieces ready to lay. Standard slab gauges are 2 cm and 3 cm; tiles and cladding are cut to your drawing. Every order is sampled and shade-matched before it ships, and bundled by block so veining stays continuous across a wall or floor.

01 / Marble
Marble · Signature line

Pietra Grey

a.k.a. Persian Grey · Negro / Grafito Marquina alternative

A deep charcoal-graphite marble crossed by clean white veins. Bookmatched, it produces the dramatic mirrored feature walls people associate with luxury hospitality; honed flat, it becomes a quiet, architectural floor. Dense and even-grained, it polishes to a high gloss and takes a leathered finish beautifully. Our most-requested stone for Armenian interiors.

02 / Travertine
Travertine · The workhorse

Persian Cream Travertine

Hajiabad / Abbasabad beds · Yazd & Markazi

Warm, light and remarkably consistent — the stone that clads more facades than any other we ship. Cut against the bed (cross-cut) it reads as a soft cloudy field; cut along it (vein-cut) it shows linear movement. Supplied filled and honed for interiors, or unfilled and brushed for grip outside. Frost-tolerant and easy to maintain.

03 / Marble
Marble · The classic

Marsh White Marble

a.k.a. Persian White · Neyriz white

A bright, creamy white marble with soft grey and faint golden veining — Iran's answer to the classic European whites, at a price that lets you use it generously. Luminous when polished, calm when honed. A natural choice for lobbies, stairs and bathrooms that need to feel light.

04 / Onyx
Onyx · The statement

Honey Onyx

Translucent · backlit grade

The luxury stone in the range. Onyx is translucent, so a panel lit from behind glows in amber and caramel layers — the detail that makes a reception desk or a bar front unforgettable. We supply it resin-backed and mesh-reinforced for strength, bookmatched for symmetry, and always sampled against your own lighting before production.

05 / Onyx
Onyx · The jewel

Green Onyx

Emerald / jade translucent

Deep emerald and jade, threaded with white and gold. Rarer and more theatrical than honey onyx, it suits smaller, high-impact surfaces — a tabletop, a powder-room wall, a feature niche — where a little goes a long way. Handled and reinforced exactly as the honey grade.
