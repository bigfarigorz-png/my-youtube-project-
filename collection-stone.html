Why Iranian stone.

Iran is one of the world's great stone countries — vast reserves, hundreds of working quarries, and varieties that the trade has prized for generations. The advantage for an Armenian project is simple geography: this material is next door, not an ocean away.

We supply stone two ways — as gangsaw slabs for fabricators who cut to size, and as calibrated tiles and cut-to-size pieces ready to lay. Standard slab gauges are 2 cm and 3 cm; tiles and cladding are cut to your drawing. Every order is sampled and shade-matched before it ships, and bundled by block so veining stays continuous across a wall or floor.

01 / Onyx
Onyx · The statement

Golden Onyx

Vein-cut · honey & amber banding · backlit grade

The showpiece of the yard. Vein-cut to reveal long honey-and-amber bands, this onyx is translucent — lit from behind it glows, turning a reception desk, a bar front or a feature wall into the thing people remember. We supply it resin-backed and mesh-reinforced for strength, and bookmatched so a pair mirrors into one continuous image. Every panel is sampled against your own lighting before production.

02 / Marble
Marble · The character piece

Green Marble

Sage & jade ground · golden veining

A soft sage-and-jade marble shot through with warm golden veins — quieter than onyx, but with real depth and movement. It suits projects that want colour without drama: a powder-room wall, a feature floor, a run of cladding that catches the light differently through the day. Polishes to a gentle sheen and hones to a matt, contemporary surface.

03 / Marble
Marble · The all-rounder

Grey Marble

Even grey ground · soft white movement

A calm, even grey marble with subtle lighter movement — the dependable choice for large floors and walls that need to feel composed rather than busy. It reads as a single tone from across a room, then rewards a closer look. Polished for interiors, honed for a more architectural, matt finish. Slabs bundle well, so large areas stay consistent.

04 / Marble
Marble · The classic

White Marble

Bright cream-white · grey vein

A bright, creamy white marble with soft grey veining — Iran's answer to the classic European whites, at a price that lets you use it generously. Luminous when polished, calm when honed. A natural choice for lobbies, stairs and bathrooms that need to feel light, and an easy companion to the grey and green above.

05 / Lime stone
Lime stone & travertine · The workhorse

Cream Lime Stone

Warm beige · consistent · indoor & out

Warm, light and remarkably consistent — the surface that clads more facades and floors than anything else we ship. Supplied as width-40 strips and cut-to-size as well as larger formats, filled and honed for interiors or brushed for grip outside. Frost-tolerant and easy to live with, it is the quiet background that lets the onyx and marble do the talking.

06 / Marble
Marble · High contrast

Black & White Marble

Dark ground · bold white vein · bookmatch grade

A dark, dramatic marble crossed by bold white veins. Bookmatched, it produces the mirrored feature walls people associate with luxury hospitality; cut as a single slab, it makes a striking island top or a reception front. Dense and even-grained, it polishes to a deep gloss and takes a honed or leathered finish beautifully.
